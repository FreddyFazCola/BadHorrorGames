# Arcaneum — Story Bible (v0.1)

Working title: **Arcaneum**. Fully original IP — no names, characters, locations, or terminology from Harry Potter/Wizarding World. Structural inspiration only: a magic-school hub, open-world region unlocks, a faction/house system, a companion-driven main quest, and a moral-choice antagonist arc — the same narrative *shape* as Hogwarts Legacy, built from scratch.

## Logline
A latecomer to a centuries-old academy of magic discovers she can touch a forbidden second half of magic itself — and that discovery puts her at the center of a rebellion, a cult, and a 300-year-old cover-up the school's own founders orchestrated.

## World
- **Continent:** Aldreth. **Region:** Caldenwyrd, a rain-lashed highland coastline.
- **The Academy:** Arcaneum Academy of Sorcery, housed in the fortress-castle **Highhollow**, built into cliffs above the **Tidereach Sea**. Founded ~600 years ago.
- **Nearby settlement:** Thistlewick, the market town students visit on free days (Hogsmeade-equivalent, fully original layout/shops).
- **Open-world regions unlocked through the story** (mirrors Hogwarts Legacy's region-gating): the Academy grounds (hub) → the Larkmoor Fens (act 1–2) → the Ashcombe Highlands (act 2) → the Drowned Vale, a flooded ruin only reachable via Deep Weave (act 3).

## The Magic System: "the Weave"
- All magic draws on the **Common Weave**. 300 years ago, a cataclysm called **the Sundering** tore a second current out of it — the **Deep Weave** — and sealed it away as forbidden/lost.
- Casting is **Sigilcraft**: a spoken word plus a somatic gesture. Four schools, used as the gameplay spell categories: **Cantrips** (utility/exploration), **Bindings** (offense/debuff), **Wardings** (defense/shield/control), **Conjury** (summon/high-tier ultimates).
- The protagonist is one of only a handful of living people who can sense and channel the Deep Weave — the game's central hook and power fantasy, escalating across the story instead of being available from minute one.

## The Four Orders (house-equivalent, sorted at term start)
| Order | Element | Trait | Common room | Founder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember | Fire | Courage/impulsiveness | Beneath the west forge-tower | Sera Voss |
| Deep | Water | Wisdom/secrecy | Sea-cave beneath Highhollow | Aldric Thorne |
| Root | Earth | Loyalty/stubbornness | Greenhouse-terrace | Wynn Alder |
| Gale | Air | Ambition/pride | East spire, open to the sky | Corvin the Elder |

Each Order has a loyalty questline (4 side-quest chains) culminating in a decision that affects the Order's standing at the epilogue.

## The Kindred (goblin-rebellion analog, fully original)
The **Kindred** are a sentient species who alone can forge wands and casting foci, yet are legally barred from wielding one — a codified, centuries-old inequity. Three factions push on this fault line:
- **The Concord** — Kindred moderates seeking legal reform through the Crown's courts.
- **The Ashbound** — a cult/militant splinter (see antagonist) who've given up on reform and want to seize the Deep Weave by force.
- **The Academy and Crown** — institutional status quo, not cartoonishly evil, genuinely divided between reformers and traditionalists (gives the player's dialogue choices real weight rather than a binary).

## Principal Cast
- **Protagonist (player-named/customized):** A 16-year-old transfer admitted mid-term after her Deep Weave sensitivity manifests violently and nearly kills her. Orphaned; raised outside Caldenwyrd, so she's an outsider to Academy politics — the player's in-fiction excuse for needing everything explained (standard, deliberate device).
- **Provost Yevane Marrow** — Headmistress, Order of Deep. Warm but evasive; she was present at the original Sundering-adjacent incident 30 years ago that the plot slowly uncovers. Not a villain — complicit through omission.
- **Instructor Thale Brackwater** — gruff combat-magic teacher and the player's primary combat mentor; revealed mid-game as an Ashbound defector carrying guilt over Mother Cael's radicalization.
- **Fenn Oakstave** — player's best friend, a Kindred prodigy smuggled into the Academy under a disguising charm (illegal; discovery would get them both expelled or worse). Central emotional throughline and a full companion questline.
- **Corvin Ashworth** — noble-house classmate and rival; his family's fortune comes from controlling Kindred wand-trade licenses. Antagonistic throughout Act 1–2; his arc resolves toward redemption or ruin depending on player choices in Act 3.
- **Mother Cael** — Ashbound's leader, a former Order of Deep alumna and one of Marrow's own students, 25 years ago. Believes forcibly re-fusing the Common and Deep Weave under her control is the only way to end Kindred oppression and Academy hoarding of power. A tragic, arguably-correct-about-the-problem/wrong-about-the-method antagonist, not a mustache-twirler.

## Three-Act Main Quest (see `QUEST_OUTLINE.md` for the full mission-by-mission breakdown)
1. **Act 1 — Arrival & Awakening:** admission, sorting, first Deep Weave manifestation, Fenn's secret is shared with the player, first Ashbound contact (a warning, not a fight).
2. **Act 2 — The Widening Rift:** open-world regions unlock as the player investigates the original Sundering-adjacent incident; Corvin's sabotage escalates; Brackwater's past is revealed; a companion quest per Order.
3. **Act 3 — The Second Sundering:** Mother Cael attempts to force a second Sundering at the Drowned Vale; final confrontation's outcome and epilogue slides are determined by accumulated Order reputation, Kindred-subplot choices, and whether Corvin was redeemed.

## Themes
Institutional complicity vs. individual reform vs. violent revolution; found family; the cost of power that isn't evenly distributed. Multiple valid epilogues rather than a single "good" ending — deliberately unresolved the way the Kindred question shouldn't have a clean fix.
