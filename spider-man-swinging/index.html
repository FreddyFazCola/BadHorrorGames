WEB RUSH

a homemade Spider-Man swinging prototype

Click Play, then click the city to lock your mouse.

Controls

  • W A S D Run around
  • Mouse Look / aim
  • Left Click (hold) Shoot web & swing
  • W / S Reel the web in / out while swinging
  • Space Jump — or release a swing with an upward boost
  • Space Perfect release — let go at the flat, fast part of the arc
  • S (airborne) Dive — trade height for speed, then swing out of it
  • Touch a wall Stick to it — W/S climb, A/D crawl, Space jumps off
  • Q Web-zip to your aim point
  • Right Click (hold) Aim
  • C Dodge — time it to your spidey sense for a perfect dodge
  • Shift Sprint on the ground
  • Esc Pause / unlock mouse

Controller (DualSense / any standard gamepad)

  • Left Stick Run around
  • Right Stick Look / aim
  • R2 (hold) Shoot web & swing
  • Left Stick Reel the web in / out while swinging
  • Cross ✕ Jump — or release a swing with an upward boost
  • Cross ✕ Perfect release at the bottom of the arc
  • Left Stick down (airborne) Dive
  • R1 Web-zip to your aim point
  • L2 (hold) Aim
  • Circle ○ Dodge — time it to your spidey sense for a perfect dodge
  • Square ▢ / E Punch
  • L1 / L3 Sprint on the ground
  • Options Pause

A connected gamepad gets basic rumble; a DualSense also gets R2 trigger feedback while swinging - Chrome/Edge only, no setup needed.

Works wired or wirelessly - once paired to your system over Bluetooth, this connects to it the same way. (Raw in-page Bluetooth can't reach a controller's HID reports at all - browsers block that on purpose; see the README.)
